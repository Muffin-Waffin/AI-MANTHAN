## Brand & Style

This design system fuses cyber-futurism with precision industrial metallurgy, directly derived from concentric metallic coin insignias, beveled chrome letterforms, and energetic cyan rim illumination. It speaks directly to deep-tech builders, AI researchers, algorithmic architects, and elite hackathon competitors. 

The aesthetic is built upon **Cyber-Glassmorphism and Brushed Titanium Skeuomorphism**:
- **Industrial Precision:** Heavy brushed titanium chrome, machined chamfered bevels, and micro-etched concentric borders evoking high-value physical-digital hardware medals.
- **Electric Luminescence:** Concentrated electric cyan photon back-glows and energetic circuit pulses breaking through obsidian depths.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Dark, void-like deep space surfaces paired with multi-tiered specular highlights that convey prestige, championship intensity, and high-velocity innovation.

## Colors

The chromatic structure channels the light dispersion seen across liquid mercury, polished titanium, and energized quantum circuitry.

### Primary Spectrum: Electric Cyber Cyan
- **Cyan Glow / Core Primary (`#00F0FF`):** The focal light source. Used for active state borders, primary key interactive triggers, circuit node indicators, and dynamic telemetry.
- **Deep Cerulean Pulse (`#00A8FF`):** Mid-tone gradient stops and ambient back-glows that prevent cyan clipping against dark layers.
- **Sub-circuit Blue (`#003865`):** Structural track lines, inactive toggle rails, and deep gradient dropoffs.

### Secondary Spectrum: Machined Chrome & Titanium
- **Liquid Specular Chrome (`#EEF4F8`):** Peak highlight lines, metallic rim gradients, and top-edge chamfers.
- **Brushed Titanium (`#D8E2EC`):** Primary display headings, high-contrast badges, and metallic emboss emulation.
- **Steel Mid-tone (`#8FA5BC`):** Secondary text, subtle perimeter strokes, and muted icon vectors.

### Surface & Canvas Tiers: Deep Obsidian Void
- **Canvas Base (`#06080D`):** Near-black space with cool blue undertones that anchors the interface.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0B0F17`):** Base container fill for background cards, modular grids, and navigation docks.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#101726`):** Floating dialogs, elevated metric modules, and interactive hover states.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (`#162033`):** High-priority focus tiles, popovers, and tooltips.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances forward-looking technical power with strict legibility:

- **Space Grotesk (Display & Section Headers):** Delivers clean geometry with a beveled, futuristic edge. Emulates the structural presence of the metallic emblem. High-level headers (`display-hero`, `headline-lg`) frequently employ a subtle metallic silver-to-white gradient text fill (`linear-gradient(180deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #B0C4DE 100%)`).
- **Inter (Body & Core Interface):** Provides frictionless, distortion-free readability against high-contrast dark backdrops. Neutral, dependable, and refined.
- **JetBrains Mono (System Data, Timers & Telemetry):** Used for countdown timers, coordinate data, hackathon timestamps, API status indicators, and tracking tags (`INNOVATE • CODE • TRANSFORM`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layout follows a modular command-center architecture structured on a mathematical 8px rhythm. 

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views deploy a 12-column fluid grid system with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and generous `3rem` (48px) side margins. Mobile devices switch seamlessly to a 4-column layout with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) margins.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Components maintain strict internal padding hierarchies using the space scale: `space-sm` for compact tags, `space-md` for standard buttons and inputs, and `space-xl` for dashboard cards and stage modules.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major modules and telemetry blocks leverage `space-2xl` vertical spacing to emulate clear visual physical compartments.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through multi-layered rim lighting, metallic gradients, and diffused photoluminescent cyan glow rather than conventional muddy drop shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Void):** `#06080D` canvas without elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Titanium Glass Plate):** Background `#0B0F17` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional 1px border gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(216, 226, 236, 0.25) 0%, rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08) 50%, rgba(6, 8, 13, 0.8) 100%)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Active Interactive Plates):** Background `#101726` with dual outer glow:
  - Specular rim: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
  - Radiant aura: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Overlays & Modals):** Background `#162033` with high-potency glow (`0 0 40px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.22)`), encircled by double metallic-ring border framing.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary takes inspiration from high-grade coins, circular core reactors, and chamfered hardware housings:

- **Geometry Balance:** Core cards and containers use standard rounded styling (`0.5rem` / `8px` corner radiuses) accented by 45-degree chamfered sub-elements or circular inset nodes.
- **Concentric Circular Elements:** Avatar enclosures, system status badges, and progress rings adopt absolute circular geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) reinforced with concentric multi-ring concentric strokes directly echoing the emblem seal.
- **Beveled Borders:** Form control edges and button outlines utilize continuous 1px or 1.5px metallic strokes that simulate micro-machined physical edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber (Luminous Action):** High-gloss titanium body with electric cyan base fill (`linear-gradient(180deg, #00F0FF 0%, #0094FF 100%)`), dark navy text (`#06080D`), bold weight. Outer glow of `0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.45)`. On hover, the cyan intensity flares to full luminosity with an inset white specular edge.
- **Secondary (Brushed Titanium):** Translucent obsidian base with a 1px brushed chrome gradient border (`#EEF4F8` to `#8FA5BC`). Text styled in `#D8E2EC`. On hover, border turns cyan `#00F0FF` with a subtle backdrop bloom.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Monospaced label, zero border, transparent background, highlighting with an animated cyan bottom-circuit line on interaction.

### Cards & Panels
- **Metallic Tech Slate:** Multi-layered card with `background: rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.85)` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined by a dual-tone border: bright chrome on the top-left, fading to deep circuit blue on the bottom-right.
- **Emblem Feature Card:** Incorporates subtle circular concentric circuit watermark vectors in the background at 4% cyan opacity, paired with an illuminated top-edge chamfer highlight.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** JetBrains Mono uppercase (`mono-label`), pill-shaped, enclosed in a 1px stroke. Includes a pulsing 6px radial cyan LED dot indicating live status (e.g., `LIVE STAGE`, `SYSTEM ARMED`).

### Form Inputs
- **Obsidian Recessed Inputs:** Dark inset fill (`#06080D`), 1px muted border (`rgba(143, 165, 188, 0.25)`), `Inter` text at `#D8E2EC`. 
- **Focus State:** Transitions instantly into an electric cyan halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)` with border color `#00F0FF`). Labels sit atop with `mono-label` uppercase styling.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Square with `rounded-sm` corners, styled as dark metallic plates. Active check emits an intense `#00F0FF` glyph with an ambient square perimeter glow.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric dual-ring design. When selected, the central node glows cyan inside a silver metallic outer boundary ring.

### Hackathon & Telemetry Modules
- **Countdown Matrix:** Monospaced numerical blocks enclosed in metallic bordered bevel cells with cyan numerical glow.
- **Circuit Track Dividers:** Horizontal separators accented with a central 4px square cyan node and gradient tailoffs (`transparent` -> `#00A8FF` -> `transparent`).
